Halo, Optimal Portfolio Construction Platform

  • Engineered a FastAPI backend with async SQLAlchemy and Redis caching serving real end of day market data through a pluggable data provider layer, and a React and TypeScript frontend using TanStack Query and Recharts to visualize optimization results as they compute.
  • Implemented a walk forward backtesting engine free of lookahead bias, charging transaction costs and turnover constraints against the portfolio and benchmarking against index, equal weight, and 60/40 allocations. The optimized portfolio reached roughly 0.48 Sharpe over five years against roughly 0.20 for every benchmark.

Dissecting Tree of Thoughts, an Ablation Study of ToT Search Algorithms

Spring 2025

Princeton University · COS 484 Natural Language Processing · Coauthored with Junze Xie, equal contribution
