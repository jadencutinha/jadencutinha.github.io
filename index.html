Featured Project

The system I have spent the most time on, from the convex program at its center out to the interface.

SEO Tech Developer Program · Backend Lead

PortfoliU, Optimal Portfolio Construction

A platform that answers a question most investing tools avoid. Given a universe of assets, what weights should you actually hold, and can that answer be defended mathematically? PortfoliU solves eight portfolio construction objectives as convex programs in cvxpy, estimates covariance four different ways, and proves the result on a walk forward backtest that never peeks at future prices.

I led the backend, which meant the solver, the risk models, the market data layer, the backtesting simulator, and the API contract. It was recognized as the best architectural design across every group in the cohort.
